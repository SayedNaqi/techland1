# TechLand Pathways & Services — Phase 1 Acceptance Checklist

## Phase 1: current delivery

- [x] **Distinct About and Services sections.** About explains TechLand’s brand identity and guiding principles; TechLand Services catalogs its concrete service categories, details, and relevant links. Their copy, structure, and cards are distinct rather than repeating the same offers.

- [x] **Unified brand, navigation, and languages.** Combine the supplied TechLand Brand & Services brief and opportunity-platform brief as one product: TechLand is the parent brand, opportunity discovery is the flagship, and the original software/accessories/gaming/entertainment offerings remain under TechLand Services. Keep the supplied official logo. The public site supports فارسی/دری and English; language switching updates page text, navigation, typography, direction, alignment, spacing, controls, cards, forms, and responsive layout. Navigation is responsive, keyboard-accessible, has a mobile menu, and links only to implemented pages.

- [x] **Opportunity-first homepage.** Include header, hero with “Find Scholarships and Legitimate Pathways for Your Future” and its Persian counterpart, supporting copy “Explore scholarships, educational opportunities, visa information, and humanitarian pathways from trusted sources,” primary “Find My Opportunities,” and secondary “Explore Scholarships”; sophisticated text-free education/global-opportunity visual (not airplane/travel imagery); smart search; popular scholarships; humanitarian pathways; browse by country and study level; quick-match CTA; four-step “Choose Your Preferences → Discover Opportunities → Check Requirements → Apply Through Official Sources” (do not imply a user profile/account is available in Phase 1); latest opportunities; an honest upcoming-deadline state; scam prevention; guides; trust/verification; newsletter availability state; and footer.

- [x] **Scholarship directory and details.** Implement `/scholarships` and `/scholarships/[slug]` in both locales. The directory supports search, filters, sorting, pagination, save opportunity, compare opportunity, and deadline/status indicators. Filters include Country, University, Degree Level, Field, Funding Type, Nationality, Language, IELTS, Age requirement, Deadline, and Application fee. Cards contain name, university/organization, country, degree, funding, deadline state, eligibility state, official-source state, and verification state; actions include View Details, Save, Compare. Details include Overview, About the Scholarship, Funding, Eligibility, Eligible Countries, Academic Requirements, Language Requirements, Age Requirements, Required Documents, Application Process, Important Dates, Selection Process, FAQs, Official Source, Last Verified, Disclaimer, and “Apply on Official Website” only where a verified source exists. Show “Always verify current requirements on the official website before applying.”

- [x] **Humanitarian directory and details.** Implement `/humanitarian` and a sample detail route in both locales. Cover Humanitarian Visa, Humanitarian Admission, Refugee Resettlement, Family Reunification, Protection Programs, Emergency Humanitarian Programs, Community Sponsorship, and Other Legal Pathways. Each record can describe country, program/type, general eligibility, required documents, application route, official authority/source, verification date, and warnings. Details include Program Overview, Who May Be Eligible?, Who Is Not Eligible?, How the Process Works, Required Documents, Application Process, Processing Information, Official Authority, Official Source, Last Verified, Important Notes, and Disclaimer. Do not give personalized legal conclusions; include “This information is for general guidance and does not constitute legal advice.”

- [x] **Visa information.** Implement `/visa` with Student Visa, Work Visa, Humanitarian Visa, Family Reunification, Visitor Visa, and Other Legal Pathways. Explain purpose, general requirements, typical documents, process, official authority/website verification, and important warnings. Clearly distinguish general information from personal legal advice. Do not invent country rules, agencies, official links, or current pathways.

- [x] **Guides and scam prevention.** Implement `/guides`, a sample guide-detail route, and `/scam-alert`. Include scholarship, study-abroad, visa, humanitarian, application-tip, documents, language-test, and scam-prevention categories; article cards expose honest title/category/description/read-time/date/author status. Cover fake scholarships, visa agents, embassy websites, payment requests, social accounts, acceptance letters, suspicious email addresses, and unrealistic promises. Prominently state “No legitimate platform can guarantee a visa or scholarship outcome.”

- [x] **Global search, filters, quick match, and compare.** Search across scholarships, universities, countries, humanitarian programs, visa information, and guides, with an accessible autocomplete/results interface. Phase 1 matching uses only low-sensitivity opportunity preferences, is deterministic and client-side, and is explicitly not a legal or eligibility determination. Display “This appears to be a potential match based on the information provided. Always verify eligibility using the official requirements.” Save/compare persists only demo opportunity identifiers in the current browser; disclose that it is local to this device/browser. Do not collect or transmit nationality/refugee status, financial situation, passport availability, documents, or profile data in Phase 1.

- [x] **Transparent data and verification.** The initial records are realistic but clearly labeled “Demo Scholarship” and “Demo Humanitarian Program” as demo data. Never present fake information as real; every record carries its demo state. Every real opportunity must have a source, organization, official URL, last-verified date, and verification status; never invent sources, government URLs, verified badges, real awards/programs, eligibility rules, amounts, deadlines, deadlines countdowns, or statistics. When the demo record has no source/date, display “not provided/not verified” instead of fabricating it. The repository/types are structured so real verified data can later be imported.

- [x] **Legal and trust notices.** Do not present TechLand as a visa-guarantee service, immigration law firm, government organization, embassy, or official representative. Do not use “Guaranteed Visa,” “100% Visa Approval,” “Guaranteed Scholarship,” “Get Your Visa Fast,” or similar pressure language. Include this disclaimer throughout public opportunity content: “This platform provides general informational resources about scholarships, educational opportunities, visa information, and humanitarian pathways. We are not a government agency, embassy, immigration authority, or legal representative unless explicitly stated. Eligibility, deadlines, requirements, and program availability may change. Always verify information with the official source before making decisions or submitting an application.” Preserve the original decision-authority notice: “TechLand provides information and application guidance. Admission, scholarship, visa, and humanitarian decisions are made by the relevant institutions and authorities.”

- [x] **Preserved TechLand services and contact.** Keep a distinct `/services` hub with Web & Software services (website, business/e-commerce sites, web apps, custom software, UI/UX, maintenance, software installation/configuration, technical support) and Discover → Plan → Design → Develop → Launch → Support; an accessories showcase (PC, gaming, mobile, laptop, storage/USB, networking, keyboards, mice, headsets, gaming accessories, USB devices, chargers, cables, storage and other peripherals) without cart/live stock; legitimate PC/PlayStation installation, setup, configuration, updates and troubleshooting; fictional sample Movies & Series content/search with original or rights-cleared art and no unauthorized playback/download; About/Why TechLand (Technology First, One Platform, Customer Focused, Professional Support) without unverifiable claims; portfolio examples labeled “Sample Project”; CTA; contact; and legal footer. Do not invent contact or social details. Existing contact form remains visibly disconnected, must not claim delivery, and must warn not to include sensitive documents/private information.

- [x] **Reusable architecture and Phase 1 boundaries.** Preserve React/TypeScript/Vite/Tailwind/shadcn/lucide where practical; create typed demo data, reusable cards/filters/forms/modals/tables/layouts, and an API/repository abstraction. Serve static prerendered public HTML. Keep Phase 1 server/database disabled: no authentication, user dashboard, persistent profile, application tracker, calendar reminders, email/dashboard notifications, document storage, admin, CMS, verification console, analytics, or AI matching is presented as working.

- [x] **Responsive, accessible, and searchable output.** Support desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile with purpose-built layouts, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, visible focus, accessible form labels and ARIA where required, readable type, semantic heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text, reduced-motion behavior, lazy loading, and fast initial delivery. Localize all public page content and set correct `lang`/`dir`. Each public page has meaningful initial HTML, a unique title/description and appropriate Open Graph metadata; use clean URLs and structured content. Do not invent a canonical domain, canonical URL, `og:url`, or absolute sitemap origin. Keep favicon and user-provided logo.

- [x] **Routes and delivery.** Declare the actual English/Persian page routes in `public/manus-routes.json`; prerender home, directories, detail pages, visa, guides, scam alert, quick-match, compare, services, and contact. Ensure generated paths match links and no metadata placeholders remain. Preserve the current static Vite build and cache policy unless a verified routing issue requires a change. Run typecheck/build, ESLint, and source/output route, RTL/LTR, metadata, asset, and demo-disclosure checks. Save the managed-project changes to canonical `main`; do not publish.
