# TechLand Pathways & Services — Design Direction

## Product identity

TechLand remains the parent technology and digital-services brand. Its flagship public product is a calm, trustworthy opportunity-discovery portal for scholarships, education and legitimate humanitarian/visa information. Existing software, accessories, gaming and entertainment offers remain available as a clearly secondary **TechLand Services** area. The site must not look like an immigration agency, government service, visa broker, or generic template.

## Design movement and principles

Use an editorial SaaS/academic-humanitarian aesthetic: clear information hierarchy, generous whitespace, accessible contrast, practical filtering, and visible source/verification states. Prefer plain language and neutral guidance over sales urgency. Keep sensitive topics calm and dignified; do not use fear, country stereotypes, flags, government seals, travel-agent motifs, or guaranteed outcomes. Directory cards make demo status and missing sources conspicuous. Service cards remain modular and practical.

## Color philosophy

- Primary identity: the supplied deep-navy logo field (`#000958`) and a complementary ink navy (`#0D1B34`).
- Professional blue (`#2F5FA8`) carries navigation, links, and primary actions.
- Restrained teal (`#16877E`) signals constructive progress and secondary actions.
- Pale gray-blue (`#F4F7FB`) and white form the readable primary canvas; charcoal/ink remains for the header and existing gaming/software showcase.
- Green, amber, red, and blue are reserved for meaning-bearing states such as verified/active, approaching deadline, expired/warning, and informational. Do not show a state badge unsupported by actual data.
- Keep gradients subtle and limited to hero accents; avoid excessive glow, glass, color, animation, or shadows.

## Layout paradigm

Use a responsive editorial frame with an opportunity-first hero, prominent multi-filter search, clearly grouped pathways, readable detail pages, source/verification callouts, and compact trust notices. The opportunity homepage is light and content-forward; the existing `/services/` route retains its dark/light technology contrasts and legal/sample Entertainment showcase. On mobile, use vertically ordered search controls, generous tap targets, no horizontal overflow, and real RTL flow using logical CSS rather than a mirrored English layout.

## Signature elements

1. Preserve the user's official circular TechLand logo exactly: use the existing symbol crop in compact navigation/favicon locations and the complete logo lockup where the footer requires it.
2. Use a clear source-state language on opportunity records: “DEMO — not a real opportunity,” “Official source not provided,” and no “Verified” state without evidence.
3. Use fine map/network-line motifs to suggest connected educational possibilities without maps of politically sensitive regions, flags, planes, visa stamps, or government iconography.

## Interaction and motion

Search, filter, sort, and pagination must operate on the real displayed demo records; saved and compare states contain demo IDs only and stay local to the current browser. The light Phase 1 match preview accepts only low-sensitivity opportunity preferences and says it is not an eligibility decision. Keep primary content available without motion, keyboard-visible focus, accessible labels, reduced-motion support, and honest empty/error states. No interface implies server-side saving, email delivery, account access, or official application submission.

## Typography system

Use the existing Inter family for English and Vazirmatn for Persian/Dari. Maintain legible body copy, short line lengths, accessible control sizes, clear semantic heading levels, and distinct status labels. Ensure switching between `/...` and `/fa/...` changes document language/direction, navigation, alignment, form flow and card layouts.

## Brand essence and voice

**Positioning:** TechLand helps people discover education and legitimate pathways with transparent sourcing, while continuing to offer technology and digital services under one trusted brand. Personality: **clear, careful, practical**. Sound neutral, helpful, and human; use phrasing such as “Explore available pathways” and “Check official requirements.” Never promise admission, funding, visa approval, resettlement, legal outcomes, or a match.

## Asset direction

Create one original text-free homepage hero illustration: editorial, light-background, learning materials and a connected-world opportunity metaphor, muted navy/blue/teal, human and optimistic. No airplanes, passports, flags, embassy buildings, seals, fake UI text, real award branding, or guaranteed-result symbols. Keep user-provided TechLand logo exact. Use only licensed or original artwork and clearly label entertainment samples.

## Signature brand color

Deep TechLand navy `#000958`, supported by professional blue `#2F5FA8` and restrained teal `#16877E`.
